About our Projects

Our tiered project series takes you from fundamental skills to cutting-edge robotics. Whether you're 3D printing your first hexapod, programming kinematics on a robotic arm, or deploying autonomous systems with AI, there's a build designed for your experience level! Dive in, get hands-on with ZOTbotics, and bring your engineering ideas to life!

LEVEL 1: RPi Spider Bot

Focus: Intro to manufacturing, prototyping, & Embedded Basics

Step into robotics with a build that connects foundational engineering and hands-on manufacturing from scratch.

  • Gain hands-on experience with 3D printing, laser cutting power tools, and CNC fabrication.
  • Assemble mechanical linkages.
  • Intro to programming and motor control logic featuring a Raspberry Pi.

LEVEL 2: DIY Robot Arm

Focus: Kinematics & Factory Automation

Bridge mechanical design and factory automation through precision multi-axis robotic manipulation.

  • Project focuses on forward and inverse kinematics.
  • Learning modern industrial automation and robotic manipulation being operated in the real world.
  • Grouped up in teams of 8–10 to design & develop a multi-degree-of-freedom robot arm.

LEVEL 3: SLAP

Focus: Dynamic Control & Quadruped Systems

Tackle dynamic control and complex integration on an active quadruped robot.

  • Get the opportunity to choose between three sub-teams: mechanical, electrical, & software.
  • Members can design high-load chassis structures, engineer custom leg linkages, and optimal dynamic actuation assemblies.
  • Work with reliable power distribution networks and high-current motor drivers.
